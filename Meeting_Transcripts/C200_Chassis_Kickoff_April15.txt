EverCurrent Robotics — Chassis C-200 Concept Kickoff
Date: April 15, 2026 | 10:00 AM PT
Attendees: Alex Johnson (Mech Lead), Mike Patel (Robotics), Ben Carter (Marketing), Lisa Morgan (PM), Jason Wu (Supply Chain)

--- PROJECT OVERVIEW ---
C-200 is the next-generation lightweight chassis for the field robot platform.
Design targets: <12kg frame weight, 2.5x stiffness improvement over C-100, production unit cost target <$1,200.

--- MATERIAL TRADE STUDY RESULTS ---
Three candidates evaluated: 6061-T6 Aluminum, 5052 Aluminum, Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymer (CFRP).

| Material        | Weight (est.) | Cost/unit | Lead Time | Notes                          |
|-----------------|---------------|-----------|-----------|--------------------------------|
| 6061-T6 Aluminum| 11.4 kg       | $620      | 3 weeks   | Strong supplier base, proven   |
| 5052 Aluminum   | 12.1 kg       | $480      | 2 weeks   | Cheaper, lower stiffness       |
| CFRP            | 7.2 kg        | $1,850    | 12 weeks  | Exceeds weight target, 3x cost |

--- DECISIONS MADE ---
1. MATERIAL SELECTION (APPROVED): 6061-T6 Aluminum selected for EVT phase.
   - Rationale: Meets weight target, fits within budget, established supply chain through Apex Fabrication.
   - CFRP deferred to DVT evaluation only if cost reduction pathway identified (target: <$900/unit).
   - Owner: Alex Johnson

2. DESIGN CONSTRAINTS LOCKED:
   - Max frame weight: 12 kg (EVT allowance, target <11.5 kg)
   - Mounting interface: Must maintain C-100 bolt pattern for backward compatibility with existing arm actuators.
   - IP rating target: IP54 minimum for outdoor field operation.

3. SUPPLIER SELECTION: Apex Fabrication selected for EVT prototype run (3 units).
   - Apex quote: $620/unit for 6061-T6, 4-week turnaround for first article.
   - Owner: Jason Wu | Deadline: April 22

--- OPEN RISKS ---
- CFRP cost pathway unclear. If not resolved by DVT kickoff, production unit will be aluminum.
- C-100 bolt pattern compatibility: Needs formal verification by Mike Patel before CAD freeze.
- Marketing has flagged weight as a customer differentiator — Ben Carter to provide competitive benchmark data by May 1.
