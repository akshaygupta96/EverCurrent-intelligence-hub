# EVERCURRENT FINANCE & PROCUREMENT POLICY
# FISCAL YEAR 2026

## 1. PROJECT PHASES & CASH FLOW
*   **EVT (Prototyping):** High flexibility. Budget deviations up to 20% allowed without CFO approval.
*   **PVT (Production):** **ZERO FLEXIBILITY.** Any delay in design lock affects the mass-production run and triggers penalty clauses with the CM (Contract Manufacturer).

## 2. VENDOR RISK THRESHOLDS
*   **Single-Source Risk:** Any custom part with a lead time > 4 weeks must have a documented secondary source or a 10% cash reserve for "Expedited Shipping."
