# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION: A-1 ROBOTIC ACTUATOR
# VERSION: 1.0 (CONFIDENTIAL)

## 1. PERFORMANCE LIMITS
*   **Max Payload:** 12.5 kg at full extension.
*   **Peak Torque:** 85 Nm (Newton-meters).
*   **Thermal Cutoff:** 85°C at the motor housing.

## 2. MAINTENANCE & WEAR
*   **Gearbox Type:** Harmonic Drive (Model H-40).
*   **Lubrication Interval:** 5,000 cycles or 6 months.
*   **Error Flags:** Error 0xA4 signals "Over-Torque Protection Active."
